I went to The Flats in Cleveland to take some sunrise photography. I was fascinated by this bridge, which is a [functioning swing bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center_Street_Bridge_%28Cleveland%29). On either side of the base are these large fields for the bridge to swivel over.
