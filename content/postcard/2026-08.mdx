My monthly newsletter about what I am up to, which [I send in place of social media](https://www.philipithomas.com/posts/how-to-replace-social-media-with-a-personal-newsletter):

- Concluded my trip to Japan and posted more photos to the [*Tsundoku*](/tsundoku) pop-up newsletter. I am having a lot of fun exploring photography as a hobby.
- Visited Cleveland, including a stop by [William Frederick](https://www.williamfrederickclothing.com/) clothing - an emerging designer.
- Added [SMS subscriptions](/adding-sms-support) to this site - which has gotten surprisingly positive feedback. ([Text `SUBSCRIBE` to +1 212-347-3190](sms:+12123473190?body=SUBSCRIBE)).
- I [started an ongoing photography journal called *tidbits*](/introducing-tidbits-ongoing-photo-journal) and [posted eight photos there](/tidbits).
- I [open-sourced Booklet](/open-sourcing-booklet).
- Moved my personal email from Google Workspace to iCloud, since iCloud+ includes custom-domain email. Unfortunately, importing my Google calendar triggered invitations for old events (sorry).
- I moved out of San Francisco and am returning to New York. [Hanya Yanagihara](https://www.theguardian.com/books/2017/aug/12/homes-author-hanya-yanagihara-new-york-12000-books) captures my thoughts: "*The reason you stay here is for the thrill of constantly encountering people who are smarter and more interesting than you. But almost everything else about the city – the weather, the poor infrastructure, the overpriced and mediocre food scene, the subway system, the traffic, the idea that what you do is who you are – grates.*"

## Things to share
