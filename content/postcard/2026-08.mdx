## Things to share

- **Books:** I read the first two volumes of Karl Ove Knausgaard's *My Struggle* and am continuing. [James Wood's review](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2012/08/13/total-recall) is a good companion. A line in volume two captured something I like about big cities: being alone while still surrounded by people. I also read [*Paved Paradise: How Parking Explains the World*](https://amzn.to/3SYTVbl). It is kind of wild that the U.S. devotes more square footage to parking each car than to housing each person, and I enjoyed learning about Victor Gruen.
- **Articles:** I have been thinking about [Grant Achatz's question](https://grantachatz.substack.com/p/alinea-again): when a body of work is complete, do you preserve it like a museum or start again? [Hanya Yanagihara captured my mixed feelings about New York](https://www.theguardian.com/books/2017/aug/12/homes-author-hanya-yanagihara-new-york-12000-books): the people are the reason to stay, while almost everything else grates. [Cal Newport on why reading matters](https://calnewport.com/why-reading-matters/). [The British Post Office Horizon scandal](https://cacm.acm.org/news/how-software-bugs-led-to-one-of-the-greatest-miscarriages-of-justice-in-british-history/) is a brutal example of buggy software and an institution treating its data as infallible. Kevin Kelly updated ["Better Than Free"](https://tim.blog/2026/07/17/better-than-free/) for the AI era: when copies become abundant, value moves to things that are harder to copy, such as trust, authenticity, and interpretation. I am reading Ashish Uppala's [*Supercritical*](https://www.shishyko.com/supercritical/welcome-to-supercritical), about what happens when generation accelerates faster than coordination and validation.
- **Music:** [Login.jp](https://loginjp.org/) archives everyday Japanese culture through filmed DJ sets in neighborhood shops. I have also been listening to Thomas Bangalter's [*Mirage - Ballet for 16 Dancers*](https://open.spotify.com/album/7jcfOpT19mSmqfhN4d0vIz) and Brian Eno's [*Ambient 1: Music for Airports*](https://music.apple.com/us/album/ambient-1-music-for-airports/724435863).
- **Videos:** [James Hoffmann and Lucia Solis explain coffee fermentation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Iq34gq2ihMk).
- **Podcasts:** [Emma Lawler on Rippling Automated Compliance](https://open.spotify.com/episode/4Rbg94hIzKufoHhLcS4K2T). [Derek Thompson on the antisocial century](https://open.spotify.com/episode/026POHxFcmcXbqqlRHECKV) - especially the path from isolation to a need for chaos and the image of a monk in a casino.
- **Places:** [HYUNAH Coffee Club](https://hyunah.club/about) is a Greenpoint clubhouse built around home coffee brewing, with the kind of equipment most people would never buy for themselves.
- **Newsletters:** My friend Stan started a monthly newsletter, [*Know This Place*](https://nolastan.substack.com/p/know-this-place), and gave *Postcard* a shoutout.
