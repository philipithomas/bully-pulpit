## What I did in August

- Visited Copenhagen, including [a warehouse of vintage furniture and lamps](/lamps).
- Published 10 photos to [*Tidbits*](/tidbits) since the previous *Postcard*: six in August and four more in early September.

## Things to share

- **Articles:** Craig Mod on [how much risk is a useful amount of risk](https://craigmod.com/roden/116/), comparing Tokyo's restrictions with New York's disorder and arguing that tolerance for everyday risk helps preserve creativity and public life. Erich Grunewald on [why you should almost never use AI to write anything substantive](https://www.erichgrunewald.com/posts/why-i-think-you-should-almost-never-use-ai-to-write-anything-substantive/): composing sentences is part of thinking, and outsourcing the prose can conceal vague reasoning. Andrew Sharp's [New York and the Power of the Network](https://sharptext.net/2026/new-york-and-the-power-of-the-network/) uses Citadel's investment in the city to examine how employers, talent, and supporting services reinforce one another. The [FAA is recruiting video gamers to train as air traffic controllers](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/video-gamer-air-traffic-controllers-faa-recruitment-sean-duffy/#site-header88).
- **Design:** Daniel Cook's [Mixing Games and Applications](http://lunar.lostgarden.com/Mixing_Games_and_Applications.pdf) ([working PDF](https://lostgardenhome.files.wordpress.com/2019/09/mixing_games_and_applications.pdf)) uses a hypothetical princess-rescuing application to explore how software can teach skills through exploration and feedback, borrowing levels, modular tools, inventories, and goals from games.
- **Magazines:** [Works in Progress](https://worksinprogress.co/), including Deena Mousa on [Alberta's war on rats](https://worksinprogress.co/issue/albertas-war-on-rats/) - how an ongoing control program has kept rats from establishing a resident population in the Canadian province.
- **Podcasts:** [Karl Ove Knausgård on literary freedom](https://open.spotify.com/episode/7DhysxCDdvMGTWQ4cJRQuO?si=6i-jFj6PTDapANEmkiNWNg&utm_source=copy-link), on *Conversations with Tyler*: “The act of writing is like for me a place I can go to and where I am protected somehow.”
- **Videos:** [Asma Khan: Stop Calling it Curry](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sKrKI5nAXco), on *What Now? with Trevor Noah*, about food, identity, and colonialism. Thanks to [Ayush](https://radioactivetoy.tech/) for sharing this with me.
- **Tools:** [OpenRouter's rankings](https://openrouter.ai/rankings) show which AI models process the most tokens through its service. [Industry Standard Tools](https://industrystandard.tools/) is a directory of products that became defaults in their trades.
- **Ideas:** The [Baumol effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baumol_effect): wages can rise in work with little productivity growth because those employers compete for workers with more productive industries, pushing up relative costs.
